Vaulted Logic Tools

Personal tools, used by one person.

Vaulted Logic Tools is a private, personal setup that lets the owner's own command-line assistant (Claude Code, on the owner's own computer) read and organise the owner's own Google account: Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Google Analytics, Search Console and YouTube.

Who uses it

Only the owner of this Google Cloud project. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Your data

See the privacy policy. In short: data stays on the owner's computer and is never sold or shared.
